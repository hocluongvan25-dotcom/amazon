How it works

From authorization to action in minutes

No spreadsheets, no manual exports. The platform works exclusively with Amazon's official APIs, so your data is accurate and always up to date.

1

Connect your store

The account owner clicks "Connect Amazon" and authorizes the app on Seller Central using Amazon's secure OAuth flow. Nothing is shared without your consent.

2

Data syncs automatically

Orders, inventory, listings, prices, settlements and advertising metrics flow in continuously — powered by the Selling Partner API and Amazon Advertising API.

3

Teams act on what matters

Every team opens a dashboard built for its job, with alerts that flag stockouts, lost Buy Box, wasted ad spend and account-health risks before they cost money.

Security & data protection

Your store's data, treated with care

VEXIM Ops is designed to comply with the Amazon Data Protection Policy and applicable data protection laws.

✓

Official Amazon integration only

All data comes from the official Amazon Selling Partner API and Amazon Advertising API over TLS. No scraping, no third-party data sources.

🔑

Seller-authorized access

Sellers connect their accounts via Amazon's OAuth 2.0 flow and can revoke access at any time from Seller Central. Tokens are re-authorized annually.

🔒

Encryption & minimal PII

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Buyer personal data is used only to fulfill and support orders — never sold, shared, or used for advertising.

🧑‍💻

Role-based access & audit logs

Every user sees only the stores and modules they are assigned to. All write actions (prices, listings, campaigns) are logged with before/after values.

🗑️

Deletion on deauthorization

When a seller revokes authorization, tokens are deleted immediately and store data is purged in accordance with our retention policy.

🛠️

Operated by a accountable team

Production access is limited to trained, background-checked VEXIM staff under multi-factor authentication and least-privilege permissions.
